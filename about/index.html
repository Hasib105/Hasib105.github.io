About

I am a Python Software Engineer & Applied AI/ML Developer focused on building robust backend architectures, Computer Vision pipelines, and intelligent multi-modal systems. I bridge the gap between production software engineering and applied machine learning — developing scalable microservices with FastAPI & Django, implementing real-time vision pipelines with OpenCV & YOLO, deploying Transformers for NLP & vision tasks, and orchestrating stateful Agentic workflows & Speech AI (TTS/STT).

The through-line in my work: turning AI capabilities into automation people actually run.
