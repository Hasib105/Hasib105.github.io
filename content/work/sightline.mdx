Ensuring academic integrity during remote and hybrid assessments requires reviewing hours of recorded video footage without burdening proctors and educational faculty. I engineered **Sightline** as an end-to-end platform that automates video analysis, pinpoints anomalous events, and flags academically at-risk students before the semester concludes.

## Video analysis and evidence flagging

Rather than issuing unverified automated verdicts, Sightline acts as an investigative assistant for proctors and invigilators:

- **Asynchronous Video Pipeline**: Uploaded exam recordings are processed asynchronously using **Celery workers** and Redis message brokers, extracting temporal video segments and keyframes.
- **Suspicious-Event Detection**: Computer vision and audio models detect anomalous events — such as multiple persons in frame, gaze deviations, or unauthorized device usage.
- **Timestamped Evidence References**: Invigilators receive an interactive timeline highlighting suspicious events annotated with exact timestamps, model confidence scores, and frame clips to confirm or dismiss with full human agency.

## Academic intervention and resilient architecture

The platform pairs exam integrity review with early student support:

- **At-Risk Student Identification**: Analyzes student performance trends and submission flags to alert educators to struggling students well before final grading deadlines.
- **Production-Ready Architecture**: Powered by a robust Django API, PostgreSQL relational persistence, and a responsive Next.js frontend, coordinated via Docker Compose and nginx reverse proxies.
- **Automated CI/CD**: Containerized deployments automated via GitHub Actions pipelines with automated schema migrations and model warmup routines.
