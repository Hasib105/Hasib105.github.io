High-stakes technical interviews and client discussions require rapid recall and structured articulation. I co-engineered **The Noruva AI Assistant**: an invisible, real-time desktop companion that listens to incoming audio streams, matches discussion context against personal candidate experience, and delivers live suggestions without breaking conversational flow.

## Real-time speech and context retrieval

The backend pipeline is architected for sub-second turnarounds over FastAPI:

- **Streaming Speech-to-Text**: Ingests continuous microphone and system audio channels, performing low-latency speech transcription and sentence segmentation.
- **Experience-Based Personalization**: Uploaded resumes, project histories, and case studies are parsed and indexed in a **Qdrant vector store**. Incoming questions trigger semantic similarity lookups so suggestions draw directly from genuine candidate accomplishments.
- **LLM Context Synthesis**: Synthesizes structured, concise talking points and code outlines calibrated to the speaker's background, avoiding generic or hallucinated answers.

## Invisible desktop interface & privacy engineering

The client experience is engineered across Next.js and Electron:

- **Undetectable Desktop Overlay**: An Electron overlay application renders real-time suggestions atop video meeting platforms (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams) with click-through transparency and configurable opacity.
- **Bidirectional WebSocket Backbone**: Streaming tokens flow directly from the FastAPI backend to the desktop renderer with minimal compute overhead.
- **Zero-Retention Security**: Designed with end-to-end privacy guarantees, including configurable automatic transcript purge schedules and an ephemeral zero-retention mode for confidential meetings.
