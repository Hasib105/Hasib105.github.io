Navigating visa renewals, status transitions, and immigration filings is fraught with bureaucratic friction and strict legal requirements. **Torii** is a guided immigration form-preparation platform — designed as a reliable "TurboTax for visas" — that guides applicants through the legal process while ensuring strict regulatory compliance.

## Resilient backend architecture

I built the system-of-record API on modern Python 3.13 and FastAPI:

- **Async SQLAlchemy 2 & PostgreSQL 17**: High-concurrency async data access layer handling applicant profiles, case progression, jurisdiction rulesets, and document uploads.
- **Alembic Schema Evolutions**: Automated migration pipelines managed through `uv`, ensuring database schemas stay synchronized across staging and production environments.
- **Background Task Scheduling via arq**: An async Redis worker (`arq`) powers background outbox polling, automated renewal date alerts, and government policy change monitors.

## Automated intake and legal compliance

The platform replaces static PDF templates with an active workflow:

- **Conversational Intake Engine**: Evaluates applicant eligibility dynamically based on nationality, travel history, and visa category.
- **Government Source Citations**: Every generated instruction, required document, and question explicitly references and links to the authoritative government immigration regulation it originates from.
- **Auto-Populated Form Generation**: Validates field consistency across multiple applications, eliminating redundant manual data entry and preventing contradictory submissions.
